## Upstream sync: fixed adaptation points
Port upstream changes onto the fork's own model; never take these files from upstream. Splits are upstream's model (owner decision, 2026-11-25): `TransactionSplitter` replaces a transaction with parts, child rows carrying `split_parent_id`, and soft-deletes the original; upstream's `Transaction::splits()` relation is `splitParts()` here until the old lines are gone. `TransactionSplit` lines (`Transaction::splits()`, `effectiveTransactions()`) are legacy: still read, no new ones written (merging a converted split deletes its own), turned into parts by `transactions:convert-legacy-splits`. A feature that reasons about one bank movement (recurring detection, card statements, import history) reads `Transaction::movements()`, the original, not the parts; duplicate checks keep upstream's own rule in `TransactionDuplicateMatcher` (live rows, plus a trashed original while it has parts). The `split_parent_id` column comes from the fork's own migration: never take upstream's `2026_08_24_090000_add_split_parent_id_to_transactions_table`. Files that always need a hand port: `CashflowSummaryService`, `CashflowBlockQuery`, `BudgetTransactionService`, `BudgetManagementService` and `BudgetPeriod` (mutable tracking, one-off periods), `BalanceLookup` (carry-forward only), `HandleInertiaRequests` (fork props such as `betaProviders` and the flags), `WhisperMoneyServer.php`, `edit-transaction-dialog.tsx`, `transactions/index.tsx`, the dashboard cards and `recurring/*`. Keep the flags `TransactionSplitting` and `RecurringTransactions`; `CashflowAvailableFree` and `FullImport` are gone as upstream (#1278), and the full import stays off unless `FULL_IMPORT_ENABLED` is set.

## Dates a user sees: the owner's today, not the server's
"Today", "this month" and "the current period" are the data owner's: use `User::localToday()` and its siblings, not `now()` or `today()`. A shared-space row uses its own owner's calendar. Reading it needs the owner's `timezone`, so any eager load with a column list must include it (`user:id,timezone`): without it the code silently falls back to the app's date and passes every UTC test. Test with a timezone on each side of UTC, at the owner's midnight. Stored period dates and frozen goal periods are never rewritten by a date fix. Some readers still use the server's date (the dashboard projection card, `as_of` on `get_runway` and `list_recurring_series`, the monthly summary's default month).
