# Storyline (picked by the orchestrator)

**C — "One dashboard per person" / «Un dashboard por persona»**

- Device: three people's lives. Form: split screen to one — three panes play at
  once, then collapse into one.
- First 2 s (frame 0 is the thumbnail): three blank panes, each with a person's
  name and context: a freelancer, a family with kids, someone saving for a trip.
- Beats: each person says their sentence in a neutral chat bubble → three agents
  build three dashboards at the same time that look nothing alike → the panes
  merge into one empty dashboard with "What about yours?" / «¿Y el tuyo?» → end
  card (logo performed in, whisper.money, CTA).
- Look: three ground tones, one per person, from the brand palette; real UI in
  each pane; type and accent constant.
- Sound: three music layers entering one by one, playing together at the end.

The other two storylines (A "One of a kind", B "Infinite zoom") are built by
their own agents in `videos/dashboards-one-of-a-kind/` and
`videos/dashboards-infinite-zoom/`.
