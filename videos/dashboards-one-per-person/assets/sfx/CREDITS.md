# SFX credits

The sound effects in this folder come from the HyperFrames `media-use` skill's
bundled offline library, sourced from [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/)
and used under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/),
which allows commercial and non-commercial use without attribution.

Files used: `pop.mp3`, `click-soft.mp3`, `key-press.mp3`, `whoosh-short.mp3`,
`whoosh.mp3`, `riser.mp3`, `impact-bass-1.mp3`.

The music in `../music/` is synthesized by `scripts/music.mjs` and is ours.
