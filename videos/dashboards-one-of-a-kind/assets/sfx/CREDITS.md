# Sound effects

`click.mp3`, `key-press.mp3`, `typing.mp3`, `whoosh-short.mp3` and `riser.mp3` come
from the library bundled with HyperFrames' media-use skill, sourced from
[Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/) under the
[Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) (commercial
use, modification and redistribution in derived works, no attribution required).

`click-2.mp3` and `click-3.mp3` are `click.mp3` pitched up 12% and down 8% with
ffmpeg, so the four landings don't repeat one take.

`../music/bed.mp3` is original: `videos/scripts/generate-music.mjs` re-run at 96 BPM
with a soft seeded-noise tick and a sine kick added, 40 s, encoded to MP3.
